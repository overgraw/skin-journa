What happens to skin after 40 that no one tells you about

As oestrogen levels begin to shift in your late thirties and forties, your skin undergoes a series of changes far deeper than wrinkles. Among the least discussed — but most impactful — is a dramatic change in how your skin receives topical products.

"The stratum corneum — the outermost surface layer of skin — becomes measurably more rigid and less permeable as we age," explains the science behind the GlowFusion protocol. "What was a soft, supple gateway in your twenties becomes a stiff, dense wall in your forties."

The result? The same serum you used at 34 that sank in immediately and made your skin glow? At 46, it sits on the surface. It may hydrate the very top layer for a few hours. But it never reaches the dermal depth where real change actually happens.

You have not been wasting money on bad products. You have been wasting money on good products that your skin cannot absorb.
